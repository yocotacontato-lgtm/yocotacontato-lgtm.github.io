Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 2026

1. Introduction

This Privacy Policy explains how Yocota Design School (operated by YOCOTA (PTY) LTD) collects, uses, shares and protects your personal information, in line with the Protection of Personal Information Act of South Africa (POPIA, Act 4 of 2013).

Yocota Design School is an online graphic design school, not a marketplace. Every course is created, produced, recorded and taught by our own in-house team — trained instructors, a video production and recording team, a course-organisation team and a support team. We do not host third-party courses and outside sellers cannot publish or sell on Yocota Design School.

Our Information Officer is Dalton Tirano Bernardo, director of YOCOTA (PTY) LTD. You can reach us about privacy at suport@yocotaschooldesign.online.

2. Information We Collect

  • Account & enrolment details: your name, e-mail address and phone/WhatsApp number.
  • Order details: the courses you buy, the amount, the currency (ZAR) and the date.
  • Payment information: processed by our payment provider. We never receive or store your full card number, CVV or PIN — only limited confirmation data (such as success/failure and a masked reference).
  • Usage & device data: basic information such as pages visited, approximate location (from your IP) and cookies, to run and improve the website.
  • Support messages: what you send us by e-mail or WhatsApp.

3. How We Use Your Information

  • To create your access and deliver the courses you bought;
  • To process payments and prevent fraud (with our payment provider);
  • To provide support and respond to your requests;
  • To send you essential messages about your enrolment and account;
  • To keep records we are legally required to keep (for example tax/accounting);
  • To improve our courses, website and security.

We only send marketing e-mails if you choose to receive them, and you can unsubscribe at any time.

4. Legal Basis (POPIA)

We process your information where it is necessary to perform our contract with you (delivering your courses), to comply with the law, for our legitimate interests (such as security and improving the Service) balanced against your rights, or with your consent (for example, marketing). We process only what we need.

5. How Payments Are Handled

Card payments are handled on a secure, encrypted checkout by a licensed payment service provider (Paystack). The payment provider processes your card data under its own strict security standards (PCI-DSS). Yocota Design School does not store your card details.

6. Who We Share Information With

We do not sell your personal information. We share it only with trusted parties who help us run the School, under confidentiality and data-protection obligations, including:

  • Our payment provider, to process payments securely;
  • Our e-mail/communications provider, to deliver your access and support;
  • Our hosting and website infrastructure providers;
  • Authorities or advisers where the law requires it, or to protect our lawful rights.

7. Security

We use appropriate technical and organisational measures to protect your information, including encryption in transit (HTTPS) and limiting who can access your data. No system is perfectly secure, but we take your privacy seriously and act quickly if a problem arises.

8. How Long We Keep Your Information

We keep your information for as long as you have access to your courses and for as long as the law requires us to keep records (for example, financial records). When we no longer need it, we delete or anonymise it.

9. Your Rights

Under POPIA you have the right to:

  • Ask what personal information we hold about you and get a copy;
  • Ask us to correct or update information that is wrong or incomplete;
  • Ask us to delete information we no longer have a lawful reason to keep;
  • Object to certain processing and withdraw consent (for example, marketing);
  • Complain to the Information Regulator.

To use any of these rights, contact suport@yocotaschooldesign.online. We respond free of charge and as quickly as we can.

10. Cookies

We use a small number of cookies to make the website work and to understand how it is used. See our Cookie Policy for details and how to control them.

11. Children

The Service is intended for adults. We do not knowingly collect information from children under 18 without the consent of a parent or guardian. If you believe a child has given us information, contact us and we will remove it.

12. Complaints to the Regulator

If you are not satisfied with how we handle your information, you can complain to the Information Regulator of South Africa (inforegulator.org.za), e-mail POPIAComplaints@inforegulator.org.za. We would, however, appreciate the chance to put things right first.

13. Contact

Questions about your privacy? Contact our Information Officer.
