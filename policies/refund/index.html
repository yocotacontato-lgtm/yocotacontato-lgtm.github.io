Refund Policy

Last updated: November 2026

1. Our Refund Promise

We want you to feel safe buying from Yocota Design School. This Refund Policy is written to be fair and generous, and it is in addition to — never instead of — your rights under the South African Consumer Protection Act (CPA) and the Electronic Communications and Transactions Act (ECTA).

Yocota Design School is an online graphic design school, not a marketplace. Every course is created, produced, recorded and taught by our own in-house team — trained instructors, a video production and recording team, a course-organisation team and a support team. We do not host third-party courses and outside sellers cannot publish or sell on Yocota Design School.

14 days: you may request a refund within 14 days of your purchase. 7 days, not satisfied: if a course is not right for you, request a refund within 7 days and we refund you in full, as long as you have not watched more than 30% of that course.

2. 14-Day Money-Back Window

If something went wrong — you were charged by mistake, bought the wrong course, could not access the content, or simply changed your mind — tell us within 14 days of your purchase and we will make it right, including a full refund where appropriate.

3. Not Satisfied? The 7-Day / 30% Rule

If you started a course and it is not what you hoped for, you can ask for a full refund within 7 days of purchase, provided you have watched 30% or less of that course. This lets you genuinely try a course while keeping the promise fair for both sides.

If you are just past these windows, still contact us — we review every request in good faith and will always try to find a fair solution in your favour (for example, swapping you to another course or giving credit).

4. How to Request a Refund

Requesting a refund is simple and free:

  • E-mail suport@yocotaschooldesign.online (subject: "Refund request") or message us on WhatsApp;
  • Tell us the e-mail you used to pay and, if you have it, the order reference;
  • That’s it — you do not need to give a long explanation.

5. How Quickly You Get Your Money Back

We aim to approve eligible refunds within 1–2 business days. Approved refunds are paid back to the same card/payment method you used, through our payment provider. Depending on your bank, it can take a few more business days to appear on your statement — this last step is controlled by your bank, not by us.

6. Free Courses

Free courses have no payment, so there is nothing to refund. You can simply stop using them at any time.

7. Fair Use

Refunds are meant to protect genuine students. We may decline or limit a refund only in clear cases of abuse or fraud — for example, repeatedly buying, fully consuming and then refunding courses, or fraudulent payments. Even then, we act fairly and explain our decision.

8. Card Disputes and Chargebacks

If you think there is a problem with a charge, please contact us first — we can almost always resolve it faster than a bank dispute, and usually in your favour. If you have already started a chargeback, let us know so we can help sort it out.

9. Contact

Need a refund or have a question about one? We are here to help, quickly.
