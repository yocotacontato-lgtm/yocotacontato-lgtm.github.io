Course Access & Delivery

Last updated: November 2026

1. How Your Courses Are Delivered

Yocota Design School delivers everything online — there is nothing physical to ship. As soon as your payment is confirmed, we send access to the e-mail address you used at checkout, usually within minutes.

Yocota Design School is an online graphic design school, not a marketplace. Every course is created, produced, recorded and taught by our own in-house team — trained instructors, a video production and recording team, a course-organisation team and a support team. We do not host third-party courses and outside sellers cannot publish or sell on Yocota Design School.

2. Accessing Your Courses

You access your courses from your Student area by entering the e-mail you used to pay — we send you a secure link, so there is no password to remember. You can watch on a computer, tablet or phone, whenever and wherever suits you.

3. How Long You Have Access

You keep access to the courses you buy so you can learn at your own pace and revisit lessons. Any specific access period for a course is shown on that course page before you buy.

4. What You Need

All you need is a device with a modern web browser and an internet connection. To practise, you may need the relevant design software (for example Adobe Photoshop or Lightroom); each course page tells you what is required before you enrol.

5. If Something Goes Wrong

If your access e-mail does not arrive, check your spam folder and then contact us at suport@yocotaschooldesign.online or on WhatsApp — we will sort out your access quickly. If for any reason we cannot give you the course you paid for, you are entitled to a full refund under our Refund Policy.

6. Contact

Trouble accessing a course? Contact us and we will fix it fast.
